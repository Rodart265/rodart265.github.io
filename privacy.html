Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Rodart Creatives, Malawi

Bajeti Yanga is a household budgeting app. This page explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. The same text is available inside the app under Profile, Privacy Policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect your email address for authentication, your household financial entries (income, expenses, budgets), and device information for app functionality. If you allow notifications, we also keep an address for your device, used only to send you reminders and household alerts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the budgeting features. When you use an AI feature, the app sends that request to Google's Gemini service through our own server. It can include your chat message, member names, your budget and recent spending figures, and any receipt photo you scan. We do not keep these requests ourselves; Google processes them under its own terms.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore with enforced security rules. Only authenticated members of your household can access your data in the app. Authorised Bajeti Yanga staff may view account and household information when needed to provide support and keep the service running. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is shared with household members who have your household code, and with the services that run the app: Google (Firebase for sign-in, storage and notifications, Gemini for AI features) and PayChangu for Premium payments.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time via Settings. Your data is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind, then permanently erased. Payment records are kept as required for financial record-keeping. You can also export your data as CSV.
